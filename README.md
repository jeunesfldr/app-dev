## **HARRY POTTER**
*In a world full of muggles, choose a little magic.*

*Harry Potter has always been my favorite series because it offers a kind of comfort I rarely find elsewhere—a warm, familiar world woven with magic, friendship, and life lessons that stay with me long after I close the book. Its whimsical, fantasy-filled atmosphere gives me a feeling I can’t quite put into words, a gentle pull that makes me long to return to its pages again and again. The story awakens the child in me, filling me with wonder, yet carrying a bittersweet touch of nostalgia that makes the experience even more meaningful. It’s a world where I feel at home, even if only in imagination.*

> J.K. Rowling’s choice of words is one of the main reasons why I love the series—and the books as well. When I was a child, I developed a habit of using the words I heard or read in Harry Potter. Here are my favorite terms along with their meanings:

Blimey
: A British exclamation of surprise or shock; often used by Ron.

Bloody hell
: A British slang phrase expressing frustration, disbelief, or annoyance (Ron’s signature line!).

Parseltongue
: The rare ability to speak and understand the language of snakes.

Pensieve
: A magical basin used to review and store memories.

Patronus
: A protective charm that summons a guardian shaped by positive memories.
